Who we are

Who We Are

Ignite Revolution is a non-profit organisation dedicated to helping individuals and communities in need. Founded in 2018 by Jessica Naicker, the organisation aims to bring love and hope by providing practical support to vulnerable communities.

What began with counselling students at schools has grown into wider community initiatives, supported by volunteers and local partnerships.
